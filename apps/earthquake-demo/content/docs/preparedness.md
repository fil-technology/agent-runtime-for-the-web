# What to do during shaking

Drop, cover and hold on. Get under a sturdy table if there is one, protect your
head and neck, and stay away from windows. Do not run outside during shaking —
most injuries are caused by falling objects and broken glass near building
entrances.

After strong shaking near the coast, move to high ground and stay there until
the responsible agency says it is safe.

Aftershocks follow larger earthquakes and can continue for weeks. They are
usually smaller, but an aftershock can be large enough to damage buildings
already weakened by the mainshock.
