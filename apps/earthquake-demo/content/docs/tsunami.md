# Tsunami messages

Tsunami messages are issued by national agencies and regional warning centres.
Tremor displays their messages; it never produces its own.

## What the levels mean

- **Warning** — dangerous flooding is expected or occurring. Move immediately
  inland or to high ground.
- **Watch** — a distant earthquake may produce a tsunami; stay alert for a
  possible upgrade.
- **Advisory** — strong currents or waves dangerous to those in or very near
  the water are expected. Stay out of the water and off beaches and harbours.
- **No threat / cancellation** — no dangerous waves are expected.

## Which earthquakes generate tsunamis

Large, shallow earthquakes on subduction thrust faults displace the seafloor
vertically and can generate tsunamis. Strike-slip earthquakes mostly move the
ground sideways and rarely generate significant tsunamis. Deep earthquakes very
rarely do, because the seafloor above them barely moves.

## Never wait for an official message if you are on the coast

If you feel strong shaking near the coast, or the sea suddenly withdraws, move
to high ground immediately. The first waves can arrive before any message is
issued.
