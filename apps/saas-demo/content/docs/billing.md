# Billing and invoices

Invoices are available under **Settings → Billing**. Every invoice can be
downloaded as a PDF, and receipts are emailed to the account's billing contact
on the day the payment succeeds.

## When does my plan renew?

Team and Enterprise plans renew monthly on the day of the month you first
subscribed. The renewal date for your account is shown at the top of the
Billing page.

## Changing the payment method

Open Settings → Billing and choose **Update payment method**. Cards are stored
by our payment provider; Northwind never stores card numbers.

## Cancelling

Only account admins can cancel. Cancelling stops the subscription at the end of
the current billing period — you keep access until then, and no refund is
issued for the remainder of the period.
