# Plans and limits

| Plan | Production projects | Seats | Support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 1 | 2 | Community |
| Team | 10 | 25 | Email, next business day |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Unlimited | Dedicated support engineer |

Staging projects are unlimited on every plan. Exceeding a limit does not
disable existing projects; it prevents creating new ones until you upgrade.
